Privacy policy

ScrnSht has no servers and no accounts. Your screenshots stay on your phone.

In short

The developer collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, in the app or on this website. Screenshots are saved on your phone and are never uploaded.

What stays on your phone

Your screenshots, saved in your normal photo library, and your settings and crops. Uninstalling the app removes the settings; the screenshots stay in your gallery until you delete them.

The accessibility switch

If you switch it on, ScrnSht uses Android's accessibility feature only to take the screenshot without a pop-up, and to note the name of the app in front so the file name can include it. It does not read, keep or send anything on your screen, and nothing is collected.

Screen-cast mode

Without the accessibility switch, ScrnSht uses Android's screen-cast method. Android shows a casting icon while the bubble is on. The mirror of the screen stays inside the app and is used only at the moment you take a shot.

When the app uses the internet

  • Checking for a new version. Once a day at most, when you open the app, it asks GitHub for the newest release. Only the version number is read. GitHub sees your internet address, as with any website. This can be switched off in Settings.
  • Links you tap, such as this site or another app's page, open in your browser.

Children

ScrnSht is not aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on GitHub. This policy was last updated on 21 November 2026.
