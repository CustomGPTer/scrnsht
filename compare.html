Clean screenshots, with nothing to put up with

Most floating-button screenshot apps are free to install, then show adverts. ScrnSht has no adverts, nothing to buy, and crops the bars off as it shoots. This page sets it beside two well-known screenshot apps, using only what their own Google Play listings say.

What ScrnSht does not do

ScrnSht does not take long, scrolling screenshots, record the screen or capture web pages. It needs Android 13 or later and is not on Google Play, though it tells you when a new version is out.

Where the others do more

Screen Master and Screenshot touch both take long screenshots, and Screenshot touch records the screen too. Both are on Google Play, so they update themselves.
