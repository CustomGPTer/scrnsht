First public release of ScrnSht, a floating button for clean screenshots.

- A see-through floating bubble: tap for a screenshot, press and hold for the menu. Size, opacity, colour, icon and shape are all yours to choose.
- Silent screenshots through the accessibility switch, with Android's screen-cast method as a back-up.
- Crops the status and navigation bars off as it shoots, with saved crops and extra trims.
- A thumbnail after each shot with Share, Edit and Delete, and a quick editor with crop, draw, highlight, arrows, text and blur.
- Hides itself in chosen apps, in full screen and while typing. A timer, shake to shoot, a quick-settings tile, two widgets and notification buttons.
- Checks GitHub once a day for a newer version. This can be switched off.

Needs Android 13 or later. Website: https://customgpter.github.io/scrnsht/
